## Is it free?

Yes. Castwright is open source under the MIT licence. No account, subscription or watermark.

## Does it replace OBS?

No. OBS still captures and streams your video. Castwright makes the overlays, alerts, chat and automation that go on top, and can add them to your OBS scenes for you.

## Does it work with YouTube or Kick?

No, Castwright is built for Twitch only.

## Is my data sent anywhere?

Everything is stored on your computer. Castwright only talks to Twitch (for events, chat and stream info) and to OBS. There's no telemetry.

## Can I use overlays without OBS?

Yes. Overlay links work in anything with a browser source, such as Streamlabs Desktop, vMix or XSplit. Add to OBS and the OBS automation steps need OBS.

## Can I use StreamElements custom widgets?

Many of them. Paste the HTML, CSS, JS and Fields into a **Custom code** layer. See [custom widgets](/reference/custom-widgets/) for what's supported.

## Can I use it on two computers?

Put the project folder somewhere synced (**Settings → General → Project folder**) and point both installs at it. Don't run both at the same time.

## Where are my files?

## Why does my system warn about the installer?

The builds aren't code-signed yet. See [Install](/guide/install/) for how to open it.
