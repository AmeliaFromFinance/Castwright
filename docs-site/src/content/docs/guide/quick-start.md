title: Your first overlay

---

This walks through a complete overlay: a sub goal, chat, recent events and alerts.

## 1. Create it

1. Open **Overlays** and click **New overlay**.
2. Name it, keep **1920 × 1080**, and choose **Starter layout**.
3. Click **Create overlay**. The editor opens.

## 2. Make it yours

- Click a layer on the canvas, or in the **Layers** list, to select it.
- Drag to move it. Drag the handles to resize.
- Change text, colours and fonts on the right.
- Add more with the buttons under **Add layer**.

Changes save on their own. The top bar shows **Saved**.

## 3. Try it

Click **Test** in the top bar and play a sample alert. It plays in the **Alerts** layer, exactly as it will on stream.

## 4. Add it to OBS

1. Click **←  Overlays** to go back to the list.
2. On the overlay card, click the **Add to OBS** button, pick a scene and confirm.

Castwright adds a Browser Source with the right link and size. That's it: alerts, chat and goals now update live on stream.

:::tip
Prefer doing it by hand? Click the **link** button to copy the overlay link, then add a **Browser Source** in OBS, paste it as the URL and set the width and height to match.
:::

## Before you go live

Click **Go-live check** in the status bar. It checks OBS, your overlays, Twitch and alerts, and fixes most problems in one click. See [Go-live check](/guide/go-live-check/).
